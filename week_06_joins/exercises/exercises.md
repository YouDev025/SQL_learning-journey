# Week 6 Exercises

1. Write an INNER JOIN between `orders` and `customers`.
2. Use LEFT JOIN to list all customers and their orders.
3. Show products with no matching sales using a RIGHT or LEFT join.
4. Give an example of a self join for employee managers.
5. Explain the difference between INNER JOIN and LEFT JOIN.
